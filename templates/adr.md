# ADR-NNNN: Title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** _who owns the outcome_

## Context

What forces are at play: the problem, the constraints that are not negotiable, and the facts the
decision depends on. Write it so someone joining in a year understands why this was a question
at all. Link evidence — measurements, vendor documentation, incidents — rather than asserting it.

## Options

1. **Option A** — one paragraph: how it would work, what it costs, what it is good at.
2. **Option B** — …
3. **Do nothing / keep the status quo** — always worth stating, even when it is clearly wrong.

## Decision

The option chosen, stated as something a reviewer can check against code or configuration.
Then the reasons, tied to the forces above. If the choice was close, say what tipped it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we now owe: follow-up work, risks accepted,
operational burden. Include the negative consequences — a record with none is not finished.

## Revisit when

The observable signals that should reopen this decision (load, team size, a vendor change, a
new consumer). Optional, but a decision without one tends to outlive its reasons.
